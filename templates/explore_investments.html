Where should you start?

Three common starting points, based on where you are right now.

For Students

  • Start with financial literacy.
  • Learn about savings accounts, SIPs, mutual funds, and emergency savings.
  • Focus on small investments and understanding risk.

For Working Professionals

  • Explore SIPs, mutual funds, ETFs, stocks, PPF, and retirement planning.
  • Understand long-term wealth creation.

For Freelancers & Gig Workers

  • Learn about emergency funds, flexible savings, liquid funds, FDs, and tax planning.
  • Focus on managing irregular income.

These are educational starting points, not personalized investment recommendations.
